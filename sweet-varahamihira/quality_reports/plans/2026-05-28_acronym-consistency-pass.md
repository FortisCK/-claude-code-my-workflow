# Acronym Consistency Pass

## Goal
Standardize abbreviation use in `paper/main.tex` so major abbreviations are introduced as "full term (ABBR)" at first use and abbreviated thereafter, treating the abstract and main text as separately self-contained.

## Scope
- Audit English manuscript first.
- Update `paper/main.tex` for first-use definitions, repeated full terms, and undefined abbreviations.
- Mirror meaningful wording changes in `paper/main_zh.md` only where the Chinese review text would otherwise diverge.
- Compile the paper after edits and check warnings.

## Notes
- Preserve clinical terms and figure/table compactness where repeated definitions would make captions unreadable.
- Avoid changing numerical results, citations, or scientific claims.
