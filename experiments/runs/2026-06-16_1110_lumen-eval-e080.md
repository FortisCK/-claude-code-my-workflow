## Purpose

The decisive Phase-1 test of the coronary-lumen direction: does the **sharp
single diffusion sample** beat the U-Net on coronary-lumen / perceptual metrics
(even at tied or worse MAE)? Global MAE is structurally capped; the U-Net
conditional mean is blurry, diffusion samples are sharp.

## Method

`evaluate_residual_diffusion_full_volume.py` on `diffusion_v2_residual/longrun/
epoch_080.pt`, n_samples=4, 16 steps, 128³ sliding window. Now emits FOUR
predictions per case: corrupted, U-Net init, diffusion posterior **mean** (blurry),
diffusion single **sample** (sharp, seed-fixed). New lumen metrics (in GT coronary
mask): lumen MAE/RMSE, lumen Dice (HU>200 within dilated band), lumen sharpness
(in-mask gradient magnitude), lumen CNR (lumen vs surrounding ring), lumen grad-L1
vs clean. Plus the clean reference sharpness/CNR.

## Intent (falsifiable)

WIN if the sharp single sample's lumen sharpness AND CNR are closer to clean than
U-Net's, AND it does not collapse lumen Dice/MAE — i.e. it recovers vessel
structure the U-Net blurs away. Falsification: if the sharp sample is sharper but
strictly worse on every fidelity/geometry metric (pure distortion-for-perception
with no downstream benefit), the simple metrics don't support a win and the real
arbiter becomes the frozen-segmenter Dice (Phase 2a). 2-case smoke: sample sharper
(170 vs U-Net 161, clean 215) but lower lumen Dice (0.756 vs 0.796) — amber.
