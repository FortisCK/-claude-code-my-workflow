## Interpretation

The full test100 result confirms the earlier test5 and val20 signal.

The current refinement framework is better than the frozen U-Net alone on this
full test split, but the improvement is modest:

- global MAE improves by about 0.08 HU;
- heart MAE improves by about 0.65 HU;
- boundary MAE improves by about 0.72 HU;
- boundary-gradient L1 improves by about 0.23 HU;
- PSNR and SSIM improve slightly.

The posterior mean residual by itself is much worse than the U-Net on global,
heart, boundary, PSNR, and SSIM metrics. It is only helpful in boundary-gradient
direction. This again supports the posterior-residual-refinement interpretation:
diffusion is not a replacement for the supervised U-Net here; it is a local
residual proposal source that must be reliability-gated.

## Current Conclusion

This is the strongest completed diffusion-refinement result so far.

It is now fair to say:

- U-Net + gated posterior residual diffusion refinement beats U-Net alone on
  full test100.
- The win is highly consistent across cases and artifact severities.
- The win is still too small to be the final paper-level performance story by
  itself.
- The result is valuable as a validated module and as evidence that the
  diffusion posterior contains useful local correction signal.
