## Interpretation

The supervised U-Net baseline is much stronger than expected. It is no longer
just a sanity baseline; it is currently the best deterministic correction model
by full test100 global metrics.

This changes the research story:

- Diffusion v1 is not competitive with a well-trained supervised 3D U-Net on
  deterministic reconstruction metrics.
- The paper cannot rely on "diffusion beats U-Net" as the main claim.
- Diffusion v2 needs a distinct reason to exist: residual latent modeling,
  uncertainty, posterior sampling, or better artifact-local/clinical behavior.
- The next fair comparison should rerun diffusion v1 full test100 with the new
  artifact-aware metrics, then use U-Net epoch200 EMA as the supervised baseline
  for all future v2 experiments.
