## Intent

Re-run Stage-1 KL-VAE pretraining with the **canonical medical latent-diffusion
loss recipe** (L1 + KL + LPIPS + PatchGAN), aligned to MAISI v2's published
training pipeline (NV-Generate-CTMR, NVIDIA, Apache-2.0). v1 plateaued at
recon ≈ 0.034 in [-1,1] = 70 HU mean L1 (insufficient for cardiac downstream)
because (a) no perceptual loss, (b) no adversarial loss, (c) whole-volume
192³ training forced bs=1, (d) channels too thin (32 first level).

**Falsifiable expectation:** validation **L1 ≤ 0.015 in [-1,1] (≈ 30 HU mean
L1)** by epoch 100 on the 100-case val split. SSIM and PSNR are tracked but
not the primary judge — the L1 floor is the test of whether perceptual +
adversarial actually buys us reconstruction quality.

If by epoch 30 the val L1 is still ≥ 0.030, **stop and reconsider** —
candidate causes to investigate: lr too high under warmup, perceptual_weight
too aggressive, generator-discriminator capacity mismatch, or LPIPS-squeeze
fooling itself on cardiac CT (then switch to `radimagenet_resnet50`).

If KL term collapses (latent variance → 0) before epoch 30, lower
`perceptual_weight` from 0.3 → 0.1 (the perceptual signal is dragging the
encoder past the KL prior).

If discriminator wins (disc loss < 0.1, generator's adversarial term
explodes) before epoch 20, the warmup didn't help — increase `warmup_lt10`
from 0.01 to 0.001 (slow disc start further) or freeze D for first 5 epochs.

## Outcome

**Actual result:** v2 training continued through multiple local logs under
`experiments/runs/vae_v2/`. The best 64^3 continuation checkpoint reached
`val/recon=0.013618765948340297` at epoch 245. This satisfied the original
expectation of `val/recon <= 0.015`, but the model was then superseded by a
128^3 patch finetune to improve whole-heart reconstruction fidelity.

**Successor best:** `experiments/checkpoints/vae_v2/best_val.pt` from the 128^3
finetune, epoch 445, `val/recon=0.011378614380955696`.

**WandB:** project `cardiac-vae`; successor run `nqa0ot6v`.

### What happened

The 64^3 MAISI-style v2 recipe fixed the v1 reconstruction plateau and reached
the intended validation L1 threshold. A later 128^3 finetune was used because
64^3 patches cover only part of the cardiac anatomy.

### Decision

Do not use the 64^3-only checkpoint for Stage-2 unless needed for ablation.
Use the successor 128^3 finetune card as the authoritative Stage-1 record:
`experiments/runs/2026-05-14_0411_vae-v2-128-finetune.md`.
