## Purpose (EXPLORATORY → enabler)

Build the missing downstream substrate: align ImageCAS's paired coronary GT
labels (`<id>.label.nii.gz`) into the processed 192³ frame so lumen-region
metrics can be computed. The processed npz never carried the label.

## Method

`scripts/python/generate_lumen_masks.py` (new): for each case, NN-resample the
raw coronary label onto the same `v_resampled` 1mm grid, crop with the stored
`metadata['centroid_voxel']` (== the heart centroid the pipeline used), pad 0.
Non-destructive sidecar: `data/imagecas/processed/lumen_masks/lumen_mask_<id>.npy`
(uint8 192³). No TotalSegmentator re-run, no npz modification.

## Verification

- Alignment confirmed (case 21): processed-space HU at lumen voxels (mean 147,
  p90 372, 31% > 200 HU) is **identical** to raw-space HU at label voxels →
  resample+crop did not misalign. Lumen HU 147 vs immediate 3-voxel ring −20
  (epicardial fat) → lumen lands on locally-bright tubular structures.
- The earlier "lumen < myocardium HU" was the blood-pool confound (heart_mask
  includes contrast-filled chambers). Low heart-mask overlap (~5-12%) is correct:
  coronaries are epicardial, in fat outside the TotalSegmentator heart blob.

## Outcome (test100)

100/100 masks generated, **0 empty**. Lumen voxels @1mm: min 2890, median 6395,
max 12674, mean 6613. The 1mm-downsampling-thins-vessels risk is resolved — even
the minimum is ample for Dice/fidelity metrics.

## Decision

KEEP. test100 lumen GT ready. (Generate val/train on demand for a future
lumen-aware training loss.)
