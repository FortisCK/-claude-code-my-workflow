## Intent

DPS sparse-view recon needs an unconditional clean image prior p(x); our residual-EDM is
conditioned on motion inputs and cannot serve. Train UnconditionalEDM (Karras image params:
sigma_data 0.5, sigma_max 80) over clean ImageCAS CCTA. Not a paper result on its own — it is
the prior the Stage-1 DPS-vs-ASD-POCS gate plugs into. Checkpoint every 10 epochs; gate-test
an intermediate checkpoint early (do not wait for full convergence).

## Hyperparameters

Denoiser [32,64,128,128], 2 resblocks/level, no attention, 16.8M params; patch 128^3, batch 1,
AMP, EMA 0.9995, lr 1e-4. Smoke: 128^3 fwd+bwd peak 16.0 GB, edm loss finite.
