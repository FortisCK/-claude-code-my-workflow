## Interpretation

The supervised U-Net baseline is viable and already removes most global
artifact error after only 10 epochs. It does not yet match diffusion v1 on the
artifact-local heart and boundary metrics. EMA is better than online for global
metrics in this pilot, although online has lower heart RMSE.

This is not the final baseline. The next useful run is a longer `unet_v1`
training run, then the same 5-case check before any full test100 evaluation.
