## Intent

Continue Stage-1 KL-VAE training from the 64^3 patch MAISI-style v2 model into
128^3 patch finetuning. The goal is to improve whole-heart reconstruction
fidelity before freezing the encoder for Stage-2 conditional latent diffusion /
flow matching.

The 64^3 continuation reached best `val/recon=0.013618765948340297` at epoch
245. A short 128^3 attempt from epoch 300 reached `val/recon=0.013559919763356448`
at epoch 305, then was paused for another workload. This run resumed from epoch
310 and continued to epoch 500.

## Interpretation

The 128^3 finetune improved the best validation L1 from the 64^3 best of about
0.01362 to 0.01138, a relative improvement of about 16.4%. The improvement is
large enough to use `best_val.pt` as the frozen encoder candidate for Stage-2.

The validation curve was not monotonic after epoch 445, so `epoch_500.pt` should
not replace `best_val.pt` for downstream training unless a visual or downstream
check shows a reason to prefer it.

## Decision

Use `experiments/checkpoints/vae_v2/best_val.pt` with
`code/training/configs/vae_v2_128.yaml` as the default Stage-1 encoder candidate
for the next verification pass.

Before launching real Stage-2:

1. Run visual and numeric VAE reconstruction verification on fixed validation
   and test cases.
2. Estimate latent `sigma_data` from VAE-encoded train split and update
   `code/training/configs/diffusion_v1.yaml`.
3. Point Stage-2 EDM / flow configs away from stale `vae_v1` placeholders and
   toward this VAE v2 best checkpoint.
